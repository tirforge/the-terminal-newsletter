 Satellite IoT – Connecting the Unconnected
Satellite IoT emerged as a key IT trend in late 2024,
combining Internet of Things with satellite communications
to deliver global connectivity. It enables devices to stay
online even in remote areas, unlocking innovations in smart
farming, disaster response, environmental monitoring, and
logistics. With its worldwide coverage and reliability,
Satellite IoT is shaping the future of connected systems.
