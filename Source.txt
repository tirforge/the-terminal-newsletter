Govt. Engineering College Barton Hill: 
Rising Higher, Reaching Further 
Govt. Engineering College Barton Hill (GECBH),
Thiruvananthapuram, is one of Kerala’s premier
government engineering institutions, known for its
commitment to academic excellence, innovation,
and holistic student development. Since its
establishment, the college has consistently
nurtured talented engineers equipped with the
knowledge, skills, and values required to meet the
challenges of a rapidly evolving world.
Affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological
University (KTU), GECBH offers undergraduate and
postgraduate programmes across various
engineering disciplines, supported by experienced
faculty, modern laboratories, and a vibrant 
academic environment. The institution places equal emphasis on research, innovation,
industry collaboration, entrepreneurship, and community engagement, enabling students to
grow both professionally and personally.
The Department of Information Technology at Govt. E ngineering College,
Barton Hill stands as a dynamic and forward-thinkin g academic unit
dedicated to excellence in technical education and innovation.
Established in 1999, the department has steadily gr own into a center of
academic strength, producing skilled professionals who contribute to
diverse sectors of the global IT industry.
From its inception, the department has focused on n urturing a strong
foundation in computing, problem-solving, and emerging technologies. It
offers a comprehensive B.Tech programme in Informat ion Technology,
along with postgraduate programmes in Network Engin eering and
Artificial Intelligence & Data Science. The department strives to provide a
conducive learning environment that promotes academ ic excellence,
innovation, research, and ethical values, preparing  students to contribute
meaningfully to society while meeting the evolving demands of the IT
industry.
One of the defining strengths of the department lie s in its commitment
to academic excellence and innovation. Students consistently achieve top
ranks in university examinations and go on to excel  in higher education,
research, and professional careers. The department also promotes
entrepreneurial thinking by encouraging startup ini tiatives and creative
problem-solving among students.
The faculty team comprises highly qualified and exp erienced educators,
including Ph.D. holders and alumni from prestigious  institutions such as
IITs and NITs. Their expertise and mentorship play a crucial role in
shaping students into competent IT professionals an d responsible
innovators.

From Bits to Qubits: The Big Shift
All our current computers from our laptops to super computers work
on bits, which are like tiny switches that are eith er ON (1) or OFF (0).
Everything from WhatsApp messages to Netflix videos  is built using
just these 0s and 1s.
Quantum computers, however, use something called qubits. And here’s
where things get a bit mind-bending: a qubit can be  0, 1, or both at
the same time. This strange behavior is called supe rposition. There’s
another quantum phenomenon called entanglement, where two qubits
become linked, and whatever happens to one instantl y affects the
other even if they’re far apart.
Together, these two properties allow quantum comput ers to process
information in a way that classical computers simply can’t.

Introduction
Edge AI refers to the deployment of artificial inte lligence models on edge devices
such as IoT sensors, cameras, and smartphones. Unli ke traditional cloud
computing, Edge AI processes data locally, enabling  faster decision-making and
reducing dependency on internet connectivity.
With the rise of IoT, the amount of data generated is massive. Sending all this
data to cloud servers increases latency and bandwid th usage. Edge AI solves this
problem by analyzing data at its source.
Architecture of Edge AI
Edge AI architecture consists of three main compone nts: edge devices, edge
gateways, and cloud systems. Edge devices collect a nd process data, gateways
manage communication, and cloud systems handle trai ning and storage.
Inference occurs at the edge, while model training is usually performed in the
cloud due to higher computational requirements. 
Working of Edge Al 
The process begins with data collection from sensor s. The data is then
preprocessed to remove noise and improve quality. A  trained Al model performs
inference locally and generates results instantly.
If necessary, selected data is sent to the cloud fo r further analysis and
improvement of Al models. 
Advantages
Edge Al offers real-time processing, which is essen tial for applications like
autonomous vehicles and industrial automation.
It enhances privacy as sensitive data remains on th e device. It also reduces
bandwidth usage and operational costs.
Edge Al systems are more reliable because they can function even with limited or
no internet connectivity.

Future Vision
As we look ahead, ALTIORA aspires to grow beyond a magazine
into a vibrant platform for innovation, expression, and
collaboration within the Department of Information Technology.
Our vision is to continuously encourage students to explore
ideas that bridge technology with creativity, addressing real-
world challenges with thoughtful solutions.
We aim to expand the scope of this publication by incorporating
more technical insights, research-based articles, and
interdisciplinary perspectives, while still preserving the space for
artistic and personal expression. By doing so, ALTIORA will serve
as a balanced reflection of both the analytical and creative
strengths of our students.
In the future, we envision ALTIORA as a recognized platform
that not only documents achievements and ideas but also
inspires new ones. We hope it becomes a medium where
students confidently share their voices, collaborate with peers,
and contribute meaningfully to the ever-evolving world of
technology.
With continued support and participation, ALTIORA will keep
evolving—just like the field of Information Technology itself—
driven by curiosity, innovation, and the spirit of learning.
